VIA SANELLA

A PERFORMATIVE BRAND / AN IMAGINARY STRUCTURE
Via Sanella is not a fashion brand. It is a performative act disguised as a label.

Created as a fictional system within contemporary fashion culture, it explores absurdity, repetition, and illusion inside the industry.

The project references The Fool from Tarot — a symbol of movement without certainty, creation without origin, and identity without stability.
